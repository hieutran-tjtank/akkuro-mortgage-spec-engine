Chapter 1: Business Domain & Regulatory Framework

1.1 Market Context & Intermediary Distribution

The Dutch residential mortgage ecosystem relies heavily on third-party distribution: over 75% of mortgages in the Netherlands are originated through independent certified intermediaries (Hypotheekadviseurs) rather than direct retail bank branches. Intermediaries evaluate multi-lender scenarios, compile verification dossiers, and submit structured loan applications to funding lenders (retail banks, pension funds, and insurance syndicates). The Akkuro Mortgage Platform serves as a multi-actor Loan Origination System (LOS) connecting Intermediaries, Lenders, Underwriters, and Civil-Law Notaries.

1.2 Statutory Supervisory Bodies (AFM, Nibud, NHG)

Statutory Body Mandate LOS Enforcement
AFM (Financial Markets Authority) Conduct supervision, anti-predatory lending & Wft compliance. Enforces 14-day statutory reflection window post-binding offer.
Nibud (National Budget Institute) Debt-to-income financing burden tables & borrowing capacity. Calculates max borrowing limits factoring BKR (2%) and DUO debt.
NHG (National Mortgage Guarantee) Default risk safety net operated by Stichting WEW. Applies statutory cap (€435,000 in 2024/25) & 35 bps rate discount.

1.3 Stakeholder Personas & Value Proposition

Persona Role Primary Workflow Need Pain Point Addressed
Borrower (Klant) Consumer applying for financing Transparent status tracking, digital document upload, fast offer turnaround. Opaque underwriting stages and repetitive document requests.
Intermediary Advisor (Hypotheekadviseur) Certified mortgage broker Multi-scenario loan modeling, automated dossier validation, instant intake routing. Manual document verification and back-and-forth email loops with underwriters.
Lender Underwriter (Acceptant) Credit risk decision-maker Automated statutory compliance gating, auditable risk calculations, one-click offer generation. Re-keying data between disparate banking systems and manual LTV/NHG validation.
Civil-Law Notary (Notaris) Legal closing officer Tamper-proof deed instructions (Passeeropdracht), automated fund reconciliation. Delayed escrow disbursements and unverified last-minute contract amendments.

Chapter 2: Process Architecture & Workflow Modeling

The Akkuro Platform enforces a strict 5-stage lifecycle with state-machine transition validation and Four-Eyes segregation of duties.

2.1 End-to-End Sequence Flow

The origination pipeline orchestrates five principal actors: Borrower, Intermediary Advisor, Akkuro Rule Engine, Lender Underwriter, and Civil-Law Notary across 7 discrete hand-off events:

1. [Borrower] ───────► (Financials & Preferences) ────────► [Advisor]
2. [Advisor] ────────► (Model Scenarios & Submit) ────────► [Akkuro Engine]   (DRAFT -> SUBMITTED)
3. [Advisor] ────────► (Verify 4 Mandatory Docs) ─────────► [Akkuro Engine]   (DOCUMENT_VERIFICATION)
4. [Advisor] ────────► (Promote Dossier) ─────────────────► [Underwriting]    (CREDIT_ASSESSMENT)
5. [Underwriter] ────► (Approve Credit & Lock Rate) ──────► [Akkuro Engine]   (BINDING_OFFER_ISSUED)
6. [Borrower] ───────► (Digital Signature ≤ 14 Days) ─────► [Akkuro Engine]   (ACCEPTED_BY_BORROWER)
7. [Akkuro Engine] ──► (Passeeropdracht Closing Package) ─► [Civil Notary]    (NOTARY_PASSED -> Funds Disbursed)

2.2 BPMN 2.0 5-Stage Origination Swimlanes

Stage Phase Name Primary Actor Key Actions & Deliverables Automated System Gate
Stage 1 Intake & Scenario Modeling Borrower & Advisor Collect income, debts (BKR/DUO), simulate Nibud capacity. Schema validation & preliminary sanity checks (BR-STATE-01).
Stage 2 Dossier Intake & Verification Mortgage Advisor Upload & verify Employer slip, UWV record, NWWI appraisal, Purchase deed. Hard stop: All 4 documents must be verified (BR-DOC-01).
Stage 3 Credit Assessment & Gating Lender Underwriter Four-Eyes risk audit: LTV check (100/106% EBV), NHG validation, capacity. Calculated LTV <= cap & loan <= Nibud ceiling (BR-STATE-03).
Stage 4 Binding Offer & Reflection Borrower & Lender Issue immutable Definitieve Offerte; borrower reviews terms. 14-day statutory reflection window countdown (BR-OFFER-02).
Stage 5 Notary Closing & Settlement Civil-Law Notary Receive Passeeropdracht, draft deed, execute and release funds. Escrow trust balance matched against settlement statement (BR-NOTARY-02).

Chapter 3: Functional Requirements & User Stories

1-to-1 mapping across the 5 Process Stages with Given-When-Then BDD scenarios and explicit rule traceability tags (5 Flagship Stories).

EPIC-01: Loan Scenario Modeling & Application Intake (Stage 1)

US-01: Statutory Borrowing Capacity & Debt-Burden Gating BR-NIBUD-01..04 BR-CALC-01..02

As a Mortgage Advisor, I want to input client gross incomes, fixed rate period, and existing debt obligations (DUO student debt, BKR credit lines), so that the system computes statutory maximum borrowing capacity under AFM/Nibud rules and models repayment schedules. 
Scenario: Compliant capacity calculation with debt deduction
  Given an applicant with gross annual income of €65,000
  And an outstanding DUO student loan with initial balance of €20,000 (SF35 modern system)
  And an active BKR revolving credit facility limit of €2,000
  And a mortgage interest rate of 4.0% with a 10-year fixed term
  When the advisor calculates statutory borrowing capacity
  Then the DUO debt deducts €0.35 per €100 from monthly borrowing capacity (€70.00/month) per BR-NIBUD-02
  And the BKR facility deducts 2.0% of total credit limit from monthly budget (€40.00/month) per BR-NIBUD-03
  And the system outputs the net maximum permissible mortgage amount compliant with AFM rules

EPIC-02: Dossier Intake & Document Verification (Stage 2)

US-02: Mandatory 4-Document Intake & Income Verification Gate BR-DOC-01 BR-DOC-02 BR-STATE-02

As an Underwriting Intake Engine / Verification Specialist, I want to validate that all 4 mandatory statutory documents (Employer Statement, UWV Record, NWWI Appraisal, Purchase Agreement) are verified without critical discrepancies, so that incomplete dossiers are strictly blocked from underwriting. 
Scenario: Blocking incomplete dossier from advancing to underwriting
  Given a mortgage application in "DOCUMENT_VERIFICATION" status
  And Employer Statement, UWV Record, and Purchase Agreement are marked "VERIFIED"
  But NWWI Appraisal Report is missing or in "PENDING_UPLOAD" status
  When the advisor attempts to submit the dossier to Credit Assessment
  Then the system blocks the transition with error "DOSSIER_INCOMPLETE: Appraisal Report missing" per BR-DOC-01
  And the application state remains locked in "DOCUMENT_VERIFICATION"

EPIC-03: Credit Assessment & Compliance Gating (Stage 3)

US-03: Statutory LTV & NHG Compliance Decision Gate BR-LTV-01..04 BR-NHG-01..04 BR-STATE-03

As a Lender Underwriter, I want to validate requested loan amounts against statutory LTV ceilings (100% standard vs. 106% with Energy Budget) and NHG guarantee criteria, so that I can approve compliant files or log a formal statutory rejection. 
Scenario: Compliant sustainable purchase with 106% LTV
  Given a property with appraised market value of €400,000
  And certified energy-saving measures (EBV) of €20,000
  When applicant requests a total loan amount of €420,000 (LTV = 105.0%)
  Then the underwriting engine marks LTV as compliant (<= 106.0%) per BR-LTV-03
  And automatically allocates €20,000 into a designated construction deposit (Bouwdepot)

Scenario: Underwriter issues formal rejection on non-compliant loan
  Given a requested loan amount of €450,000 exceeding statutory Nibud borrowing capacity
  When the underwriter reviews the audit scorecard and triggers "Reject Application"
  Then the application transitions to terminal state "REJECTED"

EPIC-04: Binding Offer Issuance & Statutory Reflection (Stage 4)

US-04: Immutable Binding Offer Issuance & 14-Day Reflection Window BR-OFFER-01 BR-OFFER-02 BR-STATE-04

As a Compliance Officer / Borrower, I want to generate an immutable binding mortgage contract (Definitieve Offerte) and enforce the statutory 14-calendar-day reflection period (Bedenktijd), so that the borrower can execute digital acceptance or allow the offer to expire without penalty. 
Scenario: Borrower accepts binding offer within statutory window
  Given a binding offer issued on Day 1 with a 14-calendar-day reflection timer per BR-OFFER-02
  When the borrower electronically signs the acceptance on Day 7
  Then the application status transitions to "ACCEPTED_BY_BORROWER" per BR-STATE-04
  And the system initiates the civil-law notary closing package workflow

Scenario: Statutory reflection window expires without signature
  Given an active binding offer with Day 14 reflection window elapsed
  When Day 15 arrives without borrower digital signature
  Then the system automatically transitions the offer to "EXPIRED"

EPIC-05: Notary Closing Instructions & Fund Settlement (Stage 5)

US-05: Notary Closing Package (Passeeropdracht) & Escrow Settlement BR-NOTARY-01 BR-NOTARY-02 BR-STATE-05

As an Operations Specialist / Civil-Law Notary, I want to transmit structured closing instructions (Passeeropdracht) to the notary and reconcile escrow settlement statements (Afleveringsnota) upon deed execution, so that deed execution and fund disbursement are synchronized seamlessly. 
Scenario: Automated notary instruction dispatch and escrow release
  Given an application in "ACCEPTED_BY_BORROWER" status
  When calendar reaches 10 business days prior to scheduled closing date (Passeerdatum)
  Then engine transmits structured Passeeropdracht payload to the notary office per BR-NOTARY-01
  And upon notary confirmation of deed execution (Hypotheekakte), escrow funds are disbursed
  And application state transitions to "NOTARY_PASSED" (Lifecycle Complete) per BR-STATE-05

5.6 Repayment Amortization Formulas

Annuity (BR-CALC-01): Fixed gross installment $M = P \cdot \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$. Principal share increases monotonically while interest share decreases.
Linear (BR-CALC-02): Constant principal repayment $C = P / 360$. Total installment strictly decreases every month, resulting in lowest lifetime total interest.
